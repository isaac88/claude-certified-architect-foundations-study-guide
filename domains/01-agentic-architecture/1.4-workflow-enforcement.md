# 1.4 Workflow enforcement and handoff

## The enforcement spectrum

| Control | How | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt-based guidance | “Always verify the customer first” in the system prompt | Works most of the time. Non-zero failure rate |
| Programmatic enforcement | Hooks or prerequisite gates that **block** downstream tools until prerequisites complete | Works every time |

## Exam decision rule

- Financial, security, or compliance consequences → **programmatic enforcement**.
- Low stakes (tone, formatting, style) → prompt-based guidance is enough.

The exam will offer enhanced prompts and few-shot examples as answers to refund and identity bugs. Reject them.

A routing classifier that enables or disables tools does not enforce **order**. If the bug is “refund without `get_customer`”, the tools are already available; the sequence is the problem.

## Multi-concern requests

A ticket that is both “wrong item” and “double charged” is two items. Decompose, investigate in parallel with shared context (customer ID, order ID), synthesise one resolution. Do not serialise unrelated investigations without a reason.

## Structured handoff

When escalating to a human:

The human **does not have the transcript**. The packet must be self-contained:

- Customer ID
- Conversation summary
- Root cause
- Refund amount if applicable
- Recommended action

Missing any of those is a failed handoff, even if the agent “escalated successfully”.

## Practice scenario

Production data: in **8%** of cases the support agent processes refunds without verifying account ownership. Occasional refunds hit the wrong account.

| Option | Why it fails or works |
|---|---|
| **A. Programmatic prerequisite gate** | Blocks `process_refund` until `get_customer` (or equivalent) has returned a verified ID. Deterministic. **Correct.** |
| B. Enhanced system prompt | Still probabilistic. 8% can become 3%; it will not become 0%. |
| C. Few-shot examples | Same class of control as B. Token cost, no guarantee. |
| D. Routing classifier | Changes which tools exist, not the required order of calls. |

Stake is financial. A is the only mechanism that matches the stake.

## Check questions

1. Why is 8% skip rate on identity checks enough to reject “better prompting”?
2. A human agent receives “Customer wants a refund.” What is missing from the handoff, and why does it matter?
