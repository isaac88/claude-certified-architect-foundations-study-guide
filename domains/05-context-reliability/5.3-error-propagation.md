# 5.3 Error propagation

Same four categories as 2.2 (transient, validation, business, permission). Domain 5 adds **what the coordinator must receive**.

## Structured error context

- Failure type  
- **What was attempted** (query, parameters)  
- **Partial results** gathered before death  
- **Alternative approaches** (narrower query, other corpus, skip section)

Without that, the hub can only panic or ignore.

## Two anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Harm |
|---|---|
| **Silent suppression** — empty list, `isError: false` | Coordinator thinks the answer is “nothing exists” |
| **Workflow termination** — uncaught throw kills the whole research job | Throws away every other spoke’s work |

Spokes may retry **transient** errors locally, then still propagate a rich error if they give up.

## Access failure vs valid empty (again)

| | Retry? |
|---|---|
| Could not reach the source (timeout, auth) | Consider retry, then structured failure |
| Reached the source, **no matches** | **No retry.** That is the answer |

Confusing these is 2.2 and 5.3. The exam will retest it in a research or support costume.

## Coverage annotations

Synthesis must say what is **thin**:

> “Geothermal section is limited: journal API timed out after two abstracts.”

Silent omission looks like “geothermal is unimportant” and collides with 1.2 narrow-decomposition bugs. If the brief was complete but a spoke failed, **annotate the gap**; if the brief never assigned geothermal, that is the coordinator (1.2).

## Practice

Web-search spoke times out after one useful snippet. Options: `[]` success; kill entire job; structured error with snippet + alternatives.

**Answer:** structured error. Coordinator retries a narrower query or proceeds with a coverage note.

## Check questions

1. Why include partial results in a timeout payload?
2. Synthesis never mentions tidal power. How do you tell 1.2 (never assigned) from 5.3 (spoke failed)?
