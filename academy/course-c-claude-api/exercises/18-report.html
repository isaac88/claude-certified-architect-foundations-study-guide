## Key Nutrition Tips for Marathon Training

✓ **Hydration:** 4-5 liters water daily; electrolyte drinks for runs over 90 minutes
✓ **Carb Loading (3 days pre-race):** Increase carbs to 70-80% of calories; reduce fiber intake slightly
✓ **Timing:** Eat main meals 3-4 hours before runs; easily digestible snacks 1-2 hours before
✓ **Recovery Window:** Carb + protein within 30-45 minutes post-run (especially after long runs)
✓ **Long Run Days:** Increase total calories by 300-500 kcal; prioritize quick carbs during runs
✓ **Iron:** Red meat, poultry, beans (supports oxygen transport for endurance)
✓ **Sodium:** Include salt in meals and sports drinks to maintain electrolyte balance

---

## Race Week Protocol

**3 Days Before Marathon:**
- Increase carbs to 85% of calories
- Reduce fiber intake (white rice, white bread vs. whole wheat)
- Maintain normal protein (1.5g per kg bodyweight)
- Reduce fat slightly to accommodate extra carbs
- Stay hydrated

**Day Before Marathon:**
- Eat familiar, easily digestible carb-heavy meals
- Avoid high-fiber foods, excess fat, and new foods
- Drink 500-750ml extra fluid than normal

**Race Morning (2-3 hours before start):**
- 300-400 kcal of easily digestible carbs (bagel with honey, banana, sports drink)
- 8-16 oz water

**During Marathon (if over 2.5 hours):**
- 30-60g carbs per hour (sports drink, gels, bananas)
- 500-750ml fluids per hour with electrolytes
