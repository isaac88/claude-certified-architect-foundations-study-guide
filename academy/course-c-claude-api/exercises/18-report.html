## Key Nutrition Tips for Marathon Training

✓ **Hydration:** 3-4 liters water daily; 500-750ml sports drink per hour during long runs
✓ **Carb Loading:** 3-4 days before marathon, increase carbs to 70% of calories
✓ **Timing:** Main meals 3-4 hours before runs; snacks 1-2 hours before
✓ **Recovery Window:** Carb + protein (3:1 ratio) within 30-60 minutes post-run
✓ **Long Run Fueling:** For runs over 90 minutes, consume 30-60g carbs/hour (gels, sports drinks, energy bars)
✓ **Iron & B-Vitamins:** Lean meats, whole grains, leafy greens support oxygen transport
✓ **Electrolytes:** Salt foods appropriately; consider electrolyte tablets for runs over 2 hours

---

## Carb-Loading Protocol (Race Week)
- **Days 7-4 before race:** Maintain standard plan
- **Days 3-1 before race:** Increase carbs to 80-85% of calories; reduce fiber & fat slightly
- **Race morning:** Light breakfast 3-4 hours before; focus on easily digestible carbs
