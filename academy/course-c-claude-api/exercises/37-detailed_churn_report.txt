EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Overall Churn Rate: 38.60% (193 out of 500 customers)

This analysis identifies the major drivers of customer churn using statistical 
methods including t-tests, effect size calculations (Cohen's d), and correlation 
analysis. All findings are statistically significant (p < 0.05).


MAJOR CHURN DRIVERS (Ranked by Effect Size)
================================================================================

1. CUSTOMER SERVICE INTERACTIONS (Effect Size: 0.5965 - MEDIUM TO LARGE)
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Status: RED FLAG - HIGHEST PRIORITY
   
   Finding: Churned customers had significantly MORE customer service contacts
   
   Statistics:
   • Churned customers:  3.18 interactions/year (avg)
   • Retained customers: 2.49 interactions/year (avg)
   • Difference: +27.7% more interactions for churned customers
   • P-value: < 0.000001 (highly significant)
   • Correlation with churn: +0.279
   
   Interpretation:
   This is the STRONGEST predictor of churn. Higher customer service interactions
   indicate unresolved problems, dissatisfaction, or technical issues. Customers
   who contact support frequently are much more likely to leave.
   
   Business Impact: CRITICAL
   Action Required: IMMEDIATE
   

2. TOTAL VIEWING HOURS (Effect Size: 0.5213 - MEDIUM)
   ---------------------------------------------------
   Status: WARNING - HIGH PRIORITY
   
   Finding: Churned customers watched significantly LESS content
   
   Statistics:
   • Churned customers:  66.58 hours/month (avg)
   • Retained customers: 83.22 hours/month (avg)
   • Difference: -20.0% fewer hours for churned customers
   • P-value: < 0.000001 (highly significant)
   • Correlation with churn: -0.246
   
   Interpretation:
   Low engagement is a clear warning sign. Customers who don't use the service
   extensively see less value and are more likely to cancel. This represents
   a fundamental lack of product-market fit or content appeal.
   
   Business Impact: HIGH
   Action Required: URGENT


3. BINGE WATCHING SESSIONS (Effect Size: 0.4996 - MEDIUM)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   Status: WARNING - HIGH PRIORITY
   
   Finding: Churned customers had fewer binge-watching sessions
   
   Statistics:
   • Churned customers:  6.17 sessions/month (avg)
   • Retained customers: 7.69 sessions/month (avg)
   • Difference: -19.8% fewer sessions for churned customers
   • P-value: < 0.000001 (highly significant)
   • Correlation with churn: -0.237
   
   Interpretation:
   Binge-watching indicates content that hooks users. Fewer sessions suggest
   customers aren't finding compelling content that keeps them engaged for
   extended periods.
   
   Business Impact: HIGH
   Action Required: URGENT


4. UNIQUE TITLES WATCHED (Effect Size: 0.4708 - MEDIUM)
   -----------------------------------------------------
   Status: WARNING - MEDIUM PRIORITY
   
   Finding: Churned customers explored less content variety
   
   Statistics:
   • Churned customers:  19.45 unique titles/month (avg)
   • Retained customers: 23.74 unique titles/month (avg)
   • Difference: -18.1% fewer titles for churned customers
   • P-value: < 0.000001 (highly significant)
   • Correlation with churn: -0.224
   
   Interpretation:
   Low content variety suggests either: (a) limited catalog appeal, (b) poor
   recommendation system, or (c) customers not finding content they enjoy.
   
   Business Impact: MEDIUM
   Action Required: NEEDED


5. AVERAGE SESSION DURATION (Effect Size: 0.4576 - MEDIUM)
   ---------------------------------------------------------
   Status: WARNING - MEDIUM PRIORITY
   
   Finding: Churned customers had shorter viewing sessions
   
   Statistics:
   • Churned customers:  49.42 minutes/session (avg)
   • Retained customers: 57.76 minutes/session (avg)
   • Difference: -14.4% shorter sessions for churned customers
   • P-value: 0.000001 (highly significant)
   • Correlation with churn: -0.218
   
   Interpretation:
   Shorter sessions may indicate lower quality content, more interruptions,
   or less engaging viewing experience. Customers aren't staying engaged.
   
   Business Impact: MEDIUM
   Action Required: NEEDED


6. MONTHLY COST (Effect Size: 0.2610 - SMALL)
   -------------------------------------------
   Status: MONITOR - LOW PRIORITY
   
   Finding: Churned customers paid slightly less
   
   Statistics:
   • Churned customers:  $11.18/month (avg)
   • Retained customers: $12.11/month (avg)
   • Difference: -7.7% lower cost for churned customers
   • P-value: 0.004680 (significant)
   • Correlation with churn: -0.126
   
   Interpretation:
   This reflects that Basic tier customers (who pay less) churn more.
   Price itself is not the main driver - it's the tier/value proposition.
   
   Business Impact: LOW
   Action Required: MONITOR

- Multiple customer service contacts (> 3 per year)
- Basic tier subscription
